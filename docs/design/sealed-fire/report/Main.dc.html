Summary

MingldIngl already has a voice nobody else has, gem tiers as identity, and a set of accountability mechanics that other dating apps do not dare ship. The 11 September audit, run on the real Galaxy A51 build, found that the strangeness lives in nouns and borders on top of a generic dating-app skeleton: five tabs, a photo card with Skip and Send, messenger bubbles, a settings form, three pricing cards. The thesis is explained in Guides rather than felt.

The direction keeps every token, font, knot and mechanic and spends them differently. The app becomes a place: a hearth you return to, a fire where sealed travellers wait, letters instead of a chat, a square with lanterns, a forge, a mirror. Faces are earned. Fires that go quiet visibly burn down. Every screen has one knotted hero and one forged button. Time reads in candles, bells and dawns.

The rules

Everything on the following pages follows these. They are the standard for any new screen from now on.

✦
One hero, then rows. One knotted panel per screen, the thing the screen is for. Everything else is hairline rows on the floor. No second panel, no nested card, no shadow.
✦
One forged button. On the deed that matters. Every other action is an ink link, sentence case, underlined in hairline.
✦
Chips are states, never deeds. Seals mean binding: sending a letter, pledging, sealing your mirror.
✦
Three voices of type. Blackletter for the room's name, once per screen, Latin only. Yeseva One for names, numbers, eyebrows and buttons. Alegreya for everything read; italic is the app speaking, roman is the person.
✦
Rooms you can tell apart. Fire ember, Letters ink, Square lantern, Forge soot, Mirror your gem, War Room steel.
✦
Waiting is a candle, empty is a place, wrong is an ember. Never a spinner, never red, never an apology.
✦
Ceremonies fill the screen; questions are strips. The chest and the ascension get the room. Reckoning, warning and faltering are parchment strips at the bottom.
✦
The app speaks in its own voice everywhere, including the lock screen and the Codex. Or nowhere. Not half.

The fourteen moves

Ranked by how much they change what the app is, not how it looks. Moves 3, 4, 12, 13 and 14 are cross-cutting sheets.

1 · Seek, sealed

The candidate arrives under wax: first name, age, gem, oath, district and one line show; the likeness is earned seal by seal. One forged button, one ink link, no chrome strips above the card.

2 · Chat as letters

No bubbles. A dashed thread, day headings, initials as sigils, seal-break rows inline, a wax-seal send button.

5 · Restraint

One knotted hero per screen, one forged button, everything else hairline rows and ink links.

6 · The hearth

A home scene replaces the tab bar: mirror, lantern, letters, fire, anvil. The summons budget is candle stubs.

7 · Fires that go out

The Quest Log shows each thread's fire: burning, embers, cold hearth, with plain copy on whose turn it is.

8 · The Guild House

Membership as a building you climb: the Yard, the Hall, the High Table. Same prices and perks.

9 · The Square as a plaza

A top-down cobbled square. Lit lanterns are RSVPs, the bell is the round clock, gates are the RSVP window.

10 · The keepsake card

Share my character exports a poster with gem, score, streak line and seal.

11 · Hall of Names

The leaderboard as carved stone: numerals, gem sigils, points, one torch at your row. Names stay hidden.

3 · Woodcut glyphs

One hand-cut set for the five destinations and six quests. The boards show the style; the final set is still to draw.

4 · Room colours

The six light signatures pushed until rooms are unmistakable in a screenshot.

12 · Blackletter titles

Room names in a blackletter hand, once per screen, Latin only. Mongolian titles stay in Yeseva.

13 · Time in the world's units

Countdowns become candles, bells and dawns; exact times one tap away.

14 · Notifications in the voice

The push copy table rewritten in the app's register; a copy change, not code.

Decisions needed before code

These are product decisions, not paint. Everything else in this document can be built without changing a rule.

DecisionWhat it changesRecommendation
Level-zero revealSealed Seek hides the likeness at level 0; today level 0 grants one photo.Blur the level-0 photo under the seal. No engine change; the Unsealing ceremony already fits.
Embers in the openThe Quest Log would show the ghosting judgement before it lands.Keep it. It is the accountability thesis made visible. Confirm the tone of the copy.
The hearth replaces navigationSlower than a tab bar.A one-gesture way home (swipe down, or a small hearth glyph in every header). Waves 1 to 3 do not depend on this.
Blackletter without CyrillicTwo hands for two languages.Accept for now; commission a Cyrillic cut later, or drop the move. Nothing else depends on it.

11

Gaps I found while writing this

Things the boards do not settle. None blocks wave one; all must be settled before the wave that touches them.

✦
Mongolian strings run 20 to 40 percent longer than English. Every board was drawn in English; chips, eyebrows and the plaza labels need a Mongolian pass before the widths are final.
✦
Sound and haptics are not on any board. The feedback table (six world events) should gain: seal break, candle lit, bell, fire dying. Sound stays opt-in as today.
✦
Reduced motion is respected today; the new ceremonies and the hearth's embers must keep that switch.
✦
Contrast was checked for the current palette. The blackletter face and the ember-on-dark toasts need a contrast check at their real sizes.
✦
The admin panel and the web build are out of scope. The web build renders the new screens but without Skia, as today.
✦
Accessibility labels for the new glyphs and seals are not specified; each icon needs a spoken name.
✦
The keepsake card is a new share surface: its export must never include the seeded portrait of another user, only the sharer's own.
12

Out of scope

Traditional Mongolian script (removed on purpose on 5 September). A new icon library dependency. Any change to score deltas, tier thresholds, reveal ladder or ghosting rules. The admin panel.
