Summary

MingldIngl already has a voice nobody else has, gem tiers as identity, and accountability mechanics other dating apps do not dare ship. The audit on the real Galaxy A51 build found the strangeness living in nouns and borders on top of a generic dating-app skeleton: five tabs, a photo card with Skip and Send, messenger bubbles, a settings form, three pricing cards. The thesis was explained in Guides rather than felt.

The direction keeps every token, font, knot and mechanic and spends them differently. The app is a place with a temperature: a hearth you return to under a real sky, a fire where sealed travellers wait, letters instead of a chat, a square with lanterns, a forge, a mirror, and a cave you crawl together. Fire is what is alive, answered and kept. Frost is silence, absence and what was left. The app speaks in law, and never scolds the person.

The rules

Every screen in section 4 follows these. They are the standard for any new screen from now on.

✦
One hero, then rows. One knotted panel per screen, the thing the screen is for. Everything else is hairline rows on the floor. No second panel, no nested card, no shadow.
✦
One forged button. On the deed that matters. Every other action is an ink link, sentence case, underlined in hairline. Chips are states, never deeds. Seals mean binding.
✦
Three voices of type. Blackletter for the room's name, once per screen, Latin only. Yeseva One for names, numbers, eyebrows and buttons. Alegreya for everything read; italic is the app speaking, roman is the person.
✦
The app has a temperature. Fire is what is alive, answered and kept. Frost is silence, absence and what was left. A quiet thread frosts over; a shut gate is frozen; White Moon frosts the world for three days.
✦
Every object has a material. Wax is consumed, wood and iron are used, bronze is stamped once, gold opens or rewards, parchment is written. The Hall of Honours is the only armory; nothing is found, bought or stacked.
✦
Furnace, a little. Two hotter tokens only where identity and consequence live: the Fire, the Oath, the Ascension, the Square's bell, the Hall of Names. Corners square off there; everywhere else stays gold and rounded.
✦
Law in the voice. Two words per button. Short sentences with full stops. The app commands the world and states the law; it never scolds the person. Warmth stays in the letters and on the Mirror.
✦
The sky belongs to time; the cave belongs to the Campaign. The hearth's window shows the real sky and dawn; the Ascent is a climb through it. Caves and the dragon live in the Campaign and nowhere else. No other creatures.
✦
Rooms you can tell apart. Fire ember, Letters ink, Square lantern, Forge soot, Mirror your gem, War Room steel.
✦
Waiting is a candle, empty is a place, wrong is an ember. Never a spinner, never red, never an apology. Ceremonies fill the screen; questions are parchment strips at the bottom.
✦
The voice everywhere, including the lock screen and the Codex. Or nowhere. Not half.

Every screen, start to finish

Each screen appears once, in the order a person meets it. Under each: what it does, including everything the app does today that the screen keeps.

Arrival

From the store to a character. Four screens, one SMS, no password.

The Gate

Phone number and OTP under one arch. Features: +976 entry, verify.mn mobile-originated SMS with the code and shortcode shown, open-the-SMS-app button, a five-minute candle, expiry and restart, the 150₮ notice, returning users signed in by the same gate.

The Naming

Onboarding steps 1 to 3 as candles. Features: display name, age with the 18 to 99 rule, man or woman, the one-line bio (200 letters), location found by GPS with a city picker fallback, sign out from the gate.

Your Likeness

Step 3. Features: three photos minimum with upload and delete, hold to choose the first seal, the ally's six-letter code (Recruit an Ally), Next disabled until three are in.

The Oath

Step 4. Features: the three oaths (A Bond, Fate, Kin) with their proof rule, hold the iron to swear, the warning that re-swearing restarts proof, straight to the hearth.

Home

The hearth replaces the tab bar. Everything is reachable in two taps.

The Hearth

Home. Features: five destinations as objects (mirror, lantern, letters, fire, anvil), unread letters marked, the day's judgements at dawn, the real sky in the window from the day phases, candle stubs for the daily summons budget, First Dawns on the mantel until done.

The Hearth at White Moon

Festival state. Features: Naadam and White Moon tint the knots and the square's gathering for three days; White Moon frosts the window; nothing else moves.

The Hold

The map behind the sigil, already in the app. Features: every room, lit where you have been today, passages between rooms, close and return exactly where you were; nothing is reachable only from here.

The Satchel

What you carry tonight. Features: candles left, arrows in flight, the lantern lit or not, the oath sigil and its proof, the key (membership), the ally's word, the worn honour, seals held, your card; each taps through to where it is used. Nothing is bought, found or stacked.

The Fire

Seek. The thesis screen: faces are earned.

The Fire

Features: one candidate at a time under three wax seals, first name, age, gem, oath and district, the one-line bio, Summon and Dismiss, the daily budget and the sealed queue below, the tier-based match budget, the pause-profile rule, matches by geo and compatibility as today.

Letters

Every thread, from the first sealed word to a meeting under open sky.

Letters

The Quest Log. Features: each thread's fire (burning, embers, frozen), whose turn it is, the dawn judgement, fires ordered by heat, sealed names for Town Square matches.

A thread arrives

Fated Threads. Features: a weaver's arrow with Find out and Not now, threads you wove and whether they took, call an ally with your code, the empty state.

A thread

Chat. Features: the ledger of letters with day headings and sigils, seal-break rows from the reveal ladder, the wax-seal send, realtime delivery, load earlier letters, the video button when the rite is open, woven-by banner.

Moments of a thread

Features: the first letter arrives sealed and is opened by tap, the embers warning in the thread, the severed and inactive endings that close the composer.

The Seals

The reveal strip, opened. Features: likenesses earned and still under wax, age and district chips, deep-profile chips (drinks, smokes, faith, children, lifestyle) locked for the Yard and open for the Hall and High Table, the rule that only letters both send count.

The Unsealing

Ceremony when a seal breaks. Features: the room dims, the knot cracks into embers, the blur resolves, what was gained and when the next seal opens; tap to dismiss.

Things to do together

One sheet. Features: the icebreaker, the trial, the rite, planning a meeting (locked until enough letters), the campaign, the reckoning when a meeting is due; sever, cast out and report at its foot.

Break the Ice

Features: one question for both, text or options, seal your answer, wait for theirs, both revealed together, +20, the matched flourish.

The Rune Chamber

The compatibility quiz. Features: seven runes with a progress bar, tap chooses and advances, wait for their answers, the percentage revealed, XP awarded.

The Flame Rite, before

The card's states. Features: ask, asked, they asked (accept or not yet), open, flame-tested; camera refused and could-not-connect strips.

The Flame Rite

The call. Features: Agora video with mute, camera and the ember hang-up, the candle clock, end confirmation, the award afterwards, the seal on the likeness gone for good.

The Campaign

The dungeon crawl as a cave. Features: seven rooms in order, claim spoils per room, the current room's deed, sealed rooms, the dragon at the threshold, the map that is a map.

Under Open Sky

Plan a meeting. Features: activity suggestions with +50 for both, pledges as seals (one or two on it), the partner-pledged-first rule, the no-show rule stated.

After the meeting

Features: both seals on it, send word to a friend with place, time and district, rate the place, leave a memory under your gem; the Reckoning strip asks who came the next dawn.

The Square

Town Square: a live gathering of video rounds.

The Square

Features: the next gathering's date, RSVP as lighting your lantern, everyone lit shown on the plaza, gates that close, put out your lantern to cancel, first bell and number of rounds.

The Second Bell

A round. Features: live video, the question at the bell, the countdown, Light it or Dismiss, the match on a mutual yes, report from the round, rejoin after a drop.

After

States. Features: the square closed with who you met and the lanterns lit from both sides, under way with return only if your lantern was lit, and the quiet square when no gathering is called.

The Forge

Missions and the places worth the walk.

The Forge

Missions. Features: today's three quests with icons and progress, the chest that opens when all are done, the daily reset, places worth the walk with +50, weave a thread as an ink link.

A place worth the walk

The venue page. Features: name, category, district and hours, the description, rating from pairs who met there, memorable moments left by others, the rule that both must mark it.

Meetings Sworn

The date log. Features: every sworn meeting, kept or not kept and who paid, the memory you left, the empty state pointing back to the fire.

Weave a Thread

Fated Threads, weaving. Features: two phone numbers, loose the arrow, neither told until both accept, shared honour if it takes.

The Mirror

Who you are here, and what you have earned.

The Mirror

The character sheet. Features: portrait in the tier frame, name in blackletter and Cyrillic, age, district and seat, gem and points, dawns in a row, guild rank, what's next, honours worn, links to the Ascent and the card.

The Mirror, editing

Features: what travellers see seal by seal (first: name, age, one line; second: likenesses and district; third: deep profile for paid ranks), photo add and remove, the oath re-sworn from here, seal it or leave as it was.

The Ascent

Features: the six gems as a climb through the night sky, points to the next, what each tier grants (candles a night), dawns in a row and the longest, the chronicle of deeds with gains and penalties.

The Hall of Honours

Features: nine hooks, held ones lit, deeds for the unheld, progress where countable, wear one under your name, hold for its story, a new honour ignites on the wall, feats (milestone chests) to claim.

Hall of Names

The leaderboard. Features: your district's wall carved highest to lowest with gem sigils and points, one torch at your row, names hidden by the rules of the house.

The Guild House

Membership. Features: the Yard, the Hall and the High Table with what each grants, monthly prices, billing cycle of one, three or six months with the saving, renew or climb, the current rank and its expiry.

The keepsake card

Share my character. Features: a wanted-poster image with gem, score, streak line and seal, post it or keep it; only your own portrait ever.

The War Room

Settings, and the cold end of the house.

The War Room

Features: language, signal fires (push), sound, who reaches your fire (age range), seated or away (pause), the Banished, the Guild House, change my number, the Codex, leave for now (sign out), leave the realm (delete).

The Frozen Gate

Blocked users. Features: names shut out with the dawn it happened, thaw to pardon, the warm empty gate.

The Codex

Guides, privacy and terms as one book. Features: the laws of the fire, what we keep of you in plain words then in full, the binding terms dated and versioned.

Leaving the realm

Features: the strike-your-name question with what it costs, the thirty-day candle on the Mirror while pending, keep my character to cancel, hidden from the fire and the wall meanwhile.

The system sheets

What every screen above is built from.

The kit

Three voices of type; one forged button and ink links; chips; seals; one hero panel then rows.

Fire and frost

Five tokens; what burns and what frosts; the voice of law.

Materials

Wax, wood and iron, bronze, gold, parchment. Each object has one material, drawn the same way everywhere. The forged feel comes from this, not from ornament.

Woodcut glyphs

One hand-cut set for the five destinations and six quests. The boards show the style; the final set is still to draw.

Room colours

The six light signatures pushed until rooms are unmistakable in a screenshot.

Blackletter titles

Room names in a blackletter hand, once per screen, Latin only; Mongolian titles stay in Yeseva.

Time in the world's units

Countdowns become candles, bells and dawns; exact times one tap away.

Notifications in the voice

The push copy table rewritten in the app's register.

Waiting, empty, wrong

A candle, a drawn place, an ember. Uses the copy the app already has.

Ceremonies and interruptions

Chest and ascension full-screen; reckoning, warning and faltering as bottom parchment strips.

Things that appear and leave

Rewards, honours, streaks, nudges, the offline strip, the crash screen.

The small sheets

Honour story, report, city picker, change number.

10

Gaps to settle before the wave that touches them

✦
Mongolian strings run 20 to 40 percent longer than English; chips, eyebrows and plaza labels need a Mongolian width pass before the widths are final.
✦
Sound and haptics: the feedback table gains seal break, candle lit, bell, fire dying, ice cracking. Sound stays opt-in.
✦
Reduced motion: the new ceremonies, the hearth's embers and the frost must keep that switch.
✦
Contrast: the blackletter face, ember-on-dark toasts and ice-blue on night need a check at real sizes.
✦
Accessibility labels for every glyph, seal and temperature state.
✦
The keepsake card must never export another user's portrait, only the sharer's own.
✦
The admin panel and the web build are out of scope; web renders the new screens without Skia, as today.
11

Considered and dropped

✦
Monsters and bats as a category: sketch-level creature art reads worse than none, and creatures outside the Campaign make the person on the other side read as one. The dragon stays; it already exists by name.
✦
Iron as a dial: square corners and heavier rules everywhere. Kept only where furnace is.
✦
Inventory as a system: loot, drops, a shop, stackable items, stats, combat. A bag that grows turns a dating app into hoarding. The Satchel is a view of what the rules gave you and nothing more.
✦
Traditional Mongolian script: removed on purpose on 5 September.
✦
A new icon library dependency; any change to score deltas, tier thresholds, reveal ladder or ghosting rules; the admin panel.
