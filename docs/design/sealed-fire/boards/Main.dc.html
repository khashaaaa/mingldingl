Weird enough?
MingldIngl as it runs on the Galaxy A51 today, what holds it back from standing out, and five moves that would make it unmistakable.
What already stands out
The voice. "The gatekeeper listens for your word." "13 dawns and burning."
Gem tiers as identity, the fret-patterned score bar, the campaign of rooms.
The blackletter logo, Ulzii knots, embers, the tier glow.
What drags it down
The skeleton is the generic dating app: five tabs, a photo card with Skip and Send, iMessage bubbles, a settings list, three pricing cards. The weirdness lives in nouns and borders.
Corner knots on every card of every screen, so the ornament is wallpaper.
Chat, the most-used screen, is the least in character.
The thesis is invisible on Seek: the full face shows at once, like everywhere else.
One navy-and-gold palette on every screen. Stock Google icons inside a hand-forged world. Forged buttons on everything.
Where to spend the weirdness
1  Seek: candidates arrive sealed. The face is earned.
2  Chat as a ledger of letters, not a messenger.
3  One woodcut glyph set for tabs and quests.
4  Rooms with colours you can tell apart from across the room.
5  Knots on one hero per screen. The forged button on one action.
