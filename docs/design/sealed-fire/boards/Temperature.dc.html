Fire and frost: the app has a temperature
Three dials from the fierce conversation, set as the user chose them: Law up, Furnace a little, and frost as the second pole. Fire is what is alive, answered and kept. Frost is silence, absence and what was left. Law is how the app speaks.
FURNACE · A LITTLE
Two new tokens, furnace #FF7A1A and furnace bright #FFB347, used only where identity and consequence live: the Fire, the Oath, the Ascension, the Square's bell, the Hall of Names. Glows bleed further there and corners square off. Everywhere else stays gold and rounded.
FROST · THE SECOND POLE
Three tokens, rime #E8F4FA, ice #BFE3F2, glacier #7FB6D6. Frost creeps in from the edge of a screen where silence is: a thread that froze, the shut gate, the War Room, the offline strip, a meeting not kept. White Moon turns the whole world to frost for three days and the fire burns brighter against it. Break the Ice is literal.
LAW · THE VOICE
Send summons → Summon
Let them pass → Dismiss
Seeking a Bond → A Bond
Would you light a lantern with them? → Decide.
Your turn, two dawns unanswered… → Your turn. Two dawns. Judged at the third.
Two words per button. Short sentences with full stops. The app commands the world and states the law; it never scolds the person. Warmth stays in the letters and on the Mirror.

WHAT FROSTS
