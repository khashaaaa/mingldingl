Materials: what each object is made of
The forged feel comes from consistent materials, not from ornament. Each object has one material, drawn the same way everywhere it appears: in the Satchel, on the hearth, in a thread, on the wall. The metals already exist as tokens; wax, wood and parchment are three additions.
Wax
Candles and seals. Burns down, cools, breaks. The one material that is consumed.
Wood and iron
Arrows, the gate, the hearth's mantel, the anvil. Things that are used, not spent.
Bronze
The oath sigil, the ally's word, the lantern's frame. Things stamped once and kept.
Gold
The key, the forged button, the chest, the torch at your row. Things that open or reward. The honours take the metal of their deed: bronze, silver, gold.
Parchment
Letters, the card, the Codex, the interruption strips. Things written.
WHAT THE SATCHEL IS
A view of what the rules gave you today, in one place, each object tapping through to where it is used. Reached from the hearth. No engine change: candles are the daily budget, arrows are Fated Threads, the lantern is the RSVP, the sigil is the Oath, the key is membership, the word is the referral code, the honour is the equipped title, seals are the reveal ladder, the card is the share.
WHAT IT IS NOT
Not a bag that grows. Nothing is found, dropped, bought, crafted or stacked. No stats, no combat, no shop. The Hall of Honours is the only armory. Earned by deeds, never by luck, stays the law of the house.
