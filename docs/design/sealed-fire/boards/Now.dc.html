How it is now, on the phone today
Seek
A photo card with Skip and Send. Three strips of chrome push the person to the lower half. The reveal thesis is nowhere.
Chat
Orange and navy bubbles, "Type a message", SEND. Any messenger. The most-used screen is the least in character.
Missions
Four knotted panels in a column. Stock icons for hike, coffee, cocktail. The forged button is the first thing, for the rarest action.
Character
The strongest screen: gem, fret bar, glow. Still the same composition as Missions: header, divider, stacked knotted panels.
Town Square
One card and a void. The only screen whose room colour you can see, and it is a faint brown.
The War Room
Chips helped, but it is still a settings form. "The War Room" beside "Save" and "Age Range" breaks the spell.
Guild Ranks
Three pricing cards, the pattern every subscription app uses. Knots do not make it a guild.
