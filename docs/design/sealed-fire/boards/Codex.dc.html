CHAPTER I · A PAGE
On Fires That Go Out
A fire is judged at dawn. If it is your turn and three dawns pass, the fire is yours to have let die, and your standing pays. If it was theirs, you lose nothing but the fire.
Last set down the eighth day of September. The legal text is the same words, without the illustrations.
