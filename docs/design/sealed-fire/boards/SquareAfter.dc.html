The Square
Three more states of the square on one board: closed, under way, and quiet.
THE SQUARE HAS CLOSED
You met six people tonight.
Two lanterns were lit from both sides. They wait in your letters.
Номин
a sealed one
Or: "No lantern lit from both sides this time. The next gathering will be along."
UNDER WAY
The bells are ringing without you.
RETURN TO THE SQUARE
only if your lantern was lit
The square stands quiet.
No gathering is called yet. Return when the horn sounds.
"Lantern lit" replaces "match" here only; the letters keep calling it a fire once it is one.
