Waiting, empty, wrong: the three states, in character
Every screen has them. Today's StateBlock does the job; these are its costume. One drawing per state, reused everywhere, with the copy the app already has.
WAITING
Still listening at the gate.
A candle that flickers, never a spinner. After eight seconds the line changes: "This can take a minute. Your message is on its way."
Existing keys: wait_*_still, wait_*_long
EMPTY
The square stands quiet.
An empty place drawn as a place: a cold hearth, a bare wall, a shut gate. One line naming what fills it and the way to go there.
Existing keys: *_empty_title, *_empty_sub
WRONG
Your words didn't reach the scribe.
Ember, never red. It says what failed and offers one thing to do, in the app's own words. Never apologises, never vague.
Existing keys: screen_load_error, save_error, retry
